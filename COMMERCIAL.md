# Commercial licensing

Friday is **source-available**, not open source. The code is public so you
can read it, run it at home, check what it does with your data, and change it
for yourself. It is licensed under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Free: no license needed

- Running Friday in your own home, for you and your household
- Changing it for your own use and sharing those changes on the same terms
- Hobby projects, learning, research and testing
- Use by charities, schools, public research groups and government bodies

## Paid: needs a commercial license

- Selling Friday, or a modified version of it
- Pre-installing or bundling it with hardware you sell (hubs, mini PCs,
  smart-home kits)
- Running it as a hosted or managed service for customers
- Installing it for paying clients, for example as a smart-home integrator
- Using it inside a for-profit business, or building a commercial product
  on its code

If you aren't sure whether your use counts as commercial, ask. It's usually
a quick answer.

## How to get one

Contact the maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile. Include:

- who you are and what the product or service is
- roughly how many installs or customers you expect
- whether you need changes, support or white-labelling

Licenses are available per device, per seat or as a flat fee, depending on
what fits.
